Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Digital Foresight Agency ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes the information we collect through this website and how we handle it.

Information we collect

We collect the details you choose to share through our contact and quote forms, such as your name, email address, company, phone number, and project details. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use the site, for example pages viewed and general location, through standard analytics tools.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and communicate with you about your project. We use analytics data to understand what is useful on the site and to improve it. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and third parties

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a trusted form-handling service. We may use reputable providers for hosting, analytics, and email. These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records, and no longer than necessary.

Your choices
